# Retail Sales Data Analysis & Insights

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) and business intelligence project focused on understanding retail purchasing behavior, product category performance, customer demographics, and revenue trends to drive data-backed business decisions.

---

## 📌 Business Overview

In retail, understanding transaction patterns and customer preferences is essential for inventory optimization, targeted marketing, and revenue growth.

This project analyzes transactional retail sales data to extract actionable operational insights, evaluate sales volume across product categories, and identify key customer segments.

### **Key Objectives**
- **Sales & Revenue Tracking:** Analyze total sales, average basket size, and transaction volume over time.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Examine demographic patterns (age, gender) and purchasing habits.
- **Category Performance:** Identify high-performing vs. low-performing product categories.
- **Business Recommendations:** Translate analytical findings into strategic decisions for retail operations.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Language:** Python
- **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook / VS Code
